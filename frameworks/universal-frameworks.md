# Universal Marketing Frameworks

## AARRR (Pirate Metrics)
- **Acquisition** - How do users find you?
- **Activation** - Do they have a great first experience?
- **Retention** - Do they come back?
- **Revenue** - Do they pay?
- **Referral** - Do they tell others?

## JTBD (Jobs To Be Done)
- **When** [situation/trigger]
- **I want to** [motivation/job]
- **So I can** [expected outcome]

Forces of progress:
- Push (pain with current solution)
- Pull (attraction of new solution)
- Anxiety (fear of change)
- Habit (comfort with status quo)

## Positioning (April Dunford)
1. Competitive alternatives (what would they do without you?)
2. Unique attributes (what do you have that alternatives don't?)
3. Value (what capability do those attributes enable?)
4. Target customers (who cares a lot about that value?)
5. Market category (what context makes the value obvious?)

## Message-Market Fit Levels
1. **Level 1:** You can describe what you do clearly
2. **Level 2:** You can describe it in terms your ICP uses
3. **Level 3:** Your messaging triggers emotional resonance
4. **Level 4:** Your customers describe you the way you describe yourself
5. **Level 5:** Your category = your brand

## Funnel Stages
| Stage | Goal | Key Metrics |
|-------|------|-------------|
| Awareness | Know you exist | Impressions, Reach, Brand Search |
| Interest | Engage with content | Clicks, Time on Site, Email Signups |
| Consideration | Evaluate your solution | Demo Requests, Trial Starts, Pricing Page Views |
| Decision | Choose you | Conversion Rate, Sales Velocity |
| Retention | Stay and expand | NRR, Churn, LTV |
| Advocacy | Recommend you | NPS, Referrals, Reviews |

## Growth Loop Types
- **Viral loops** - Users invite users (referrals, invites, sharing)
- **Content loops** - Users create content that attracts more users (UGC, templates, community)
- **Paid loops** - Revenue funds ads that drive more revenue (ROAS > 1)
- **Sales loops** - Customers become case studies that close more customers
- **SEO loops** - User-generated pages rank and attract more users (programmatic SEO)
- **Data loops** - More users = better product = more users (network effects)

## Copy Frameworks
- **AIDA** - Attention, Interest, Desire, Action
- **PAS** - Problem, Agitate, Solution
- **BAB** - Before, After, Bridge
- **4Ps** - Promise, Picture, Proof, Push
- **ACCA** - Awareness, Comprehension, Conviction, Action
- **QUEST** - Qualify, Understand, Educate, Stimulate, Transition

## Experimentation Framework
1. **Observe** - What does the data show?
2. **Hypothesize** - If we do X, we expect Y because Z
3. **Design** - Control vs variant, sample size, duration
4. **Execute** - Run the test with minimal confounds
5. **Analyze** - Statistical significance, practical significance
6. **Decide** - Ship, iterate, or kill

## Channel Selection Criteria
Score each channel 1-5:
- **Reach** - Can you access enough of your ICP here?
- **Cost** - Can you afford to acquire customers here?
- **Speed** - How fast can you see results?
- **Control** - How much control do you have over performance?
- **Scalability** - Can this grow 10x without breaking?
- **Defensibility** - How hard is this for competitors to copy?

## Segmentation Models
- **Firmographic** - Industry, size, revenue, location
- **Behavioral** - Usage patterns, feature adoption, engagement
- **Needs-based** - Problems they're trying to solve
- **Value-based** - Revenue potential, LTV, expansion likelihood
- **Technographic** - Tech stack, tools, platforms

## Attribution Models
- **Last-touch** - Credit to final interaction before conversion
- **First-touch** - Credit to first interaction
- **Linear** - Equal credit across all touchpoints
- **Time-decay** - More credit to recent touchpoints
- **Position-based (U-shaped)** - 40% first, 40% last, 20% middle
- **Data-driven/MMM** - Algorithmic based on actual contribution
